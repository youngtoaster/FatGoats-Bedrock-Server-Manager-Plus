Documentation & FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about system requirements, data preservation, and hosting.

How does the 1-Click Addon Auto-Installer work, and why is it so fast?

Zero technical setup. Normally, adding mods and addons to Minecraft Bedrock Dedicated Server is painful: you have to extract zip archives, dig through nested folders, open manifest.json in a text editor to hunt down 36-character UUID strings and version headers, manually copy files into bds/behavior_packs/ and bds/resource_packs/, and format JSON in world_behavior_packs.json (where a single comma error crashes your server).

With FatGoats BSM+, you simply drag and drop any .mcaddon, .mcpack, or .zip file into the panel. In less than 1 second, the engine decompresses the archive, automatically separates Behavior and Resource packs, extracts high-res icons and localized names from en_US.lang, pairs companion packs into sync groups, and injects verified UUID manifests directly into your active world. It is instantaneous, 100% automated, and completely crash-proof.

Will updating the manager delete my Minecraft worlds or databases?

Never. The in-app updater and installer strictly isolate and safeguard your worlds/, data/db/, server.properties, allowlist.json, and permissions.json. Updates only replace application code and client dashboard bundles.

How does Instant Heal and Food Restore work?

FatGoats BSM+ pipes instant, amplifier-255 potion effects (heal and saturation) directly into the Bedrock Dedicated Server standard input pipe targeted at the player's exact username. It applies in under 20 milliseconds without restarting or requiring player relog.

Can players on Xbox, PlayStation, or Nintendo Switch join my server?

Yes! FatGoats BSM+ includes built-in Xbox Live Friend Bridging (via MCXboxBroadcast). When configured with your Microsoft account, the server broadcasts itself to your Xbox Friends List, allowing console players to join directly from their Friends tab without needing custom DNS settings.

Do I need to forward UDP port 19132 on my home router?

Not if you use our built-in Playit Tunnel integration. The manager creates a secure outbound encrypted tunnel to Playit's global network, assigning you a dedicated public hostname and port that bypasses CGNAT and firewalls with zero router configuration.

How are backups prevented from corrupting during gameplay?

FatGoats BSM+ strictly follows Mojang's official LevelDB backup specification. It issues save hold, polls save query until the BDS engine flushes and locks chunk files, copies only the safe snapshot files, and executes save resume before archiving to zip.
